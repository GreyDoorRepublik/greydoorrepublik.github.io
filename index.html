01 ABOUT THE STUDIO

WELCOME TO THE REPUBLIK

THE ORDINARY
IS OVERRATED.

Grey Door Republik is an independent game development studio based in the United Kingdom, creating unusual interactive experiences, unconventional stories and games that explore the space between the familiar and the unsettling.

We make games that reward curiosity, encourage exploration and occasionally leave players wondering whether they've discovered something they weren't meant to find.

Our inspiration comes from Britain's industrial and institutional past: ageing machinery, analogue equipment, forgotten workplaces, bureaucratic absurdity and the imperfect character of software from another era.

“The most interesting discoveries often begin with something perfectly normal.”
DEBUT PROJECT / IN BETA

INTRODUCING

FLOPPY//144

ONE DISK. ONE DEPARTMENT. TOO MANY SECRETS.

Enter the Government Department of Records, where procedures, forgotten information and quietly unsettling discoveries hide in plain sight.

A retro exploration mystery with an ambitious technical constraint: an entire game designed to fit within the capacity of a standard 1.44 MB floppy disk.
